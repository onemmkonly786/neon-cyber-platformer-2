HOW TO PLAY

Move: A/D or ←/→
Jump: W / ↑ / SPACE (double jump!)
Dash: SHIFT (uses energy)
Nova Pulse: E (permanently destroys nearby normal enemies for this sector)
Weapon fire: F (weapons are earned from bosses); Cycle weapon: Z
Shield: Q (temporary protection)
Overdrive: G (temporary speed boost)
Pause: P or ESC

Collect every orb to unlock the exit. Earn 1 star for clearing a sector, 1 extra for collecting every coin, and 1 extra for destroying every enemy and hazard. Grab credits, activate checkpoints, and use the Upgrade Lab between runs. New seeker drones appear in later sectors. Use F to shoot them or E to clear nearby threats. Press G for Overdrive.
